How it works

From POS order to delivered, in three steps

Recaho Extender sits on top of your existing Recaho POS workflow, no extra data entry required.

1

Install & connect

Add the Chrome extension and open your Recaho POS as usual. Recaho Extender reads your orders directly from the page you're already using.

2

Import & dispatch

Orders are imported into your dispatch board, where you assign a rider, attach a delivery photo, and monitor status from one screen.

3

Riders deliver, live

Assigned riders see their orders instantly in the Recaho Extender Companion app, and update delivery status as they go.
